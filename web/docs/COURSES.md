# Courses & badges (content plan, approved 2026-11-26)

A new **Courses & badges** section: vendor courses from OpenAI and Anthropic, each mapped to the roadmap phase where it fits.
- The owner chose a section of its own, with a curated set (2026-11-26).
- Each course has a tick, a note, a link, a badge chip where the provider says one is awarded, and its phase.
- Item ids are `cr{n}`, in the order below. They are append-only like `pp{n}` and `bk{n}`: never reorder or delete, only append; hide with a flag.

**Sources** (read 2026-11-26 from the live pages):
- https://developers.openai.com/learn and its three `/tracks/…` pages: length and level are given; no badge is mentioned.
- https://academy.openai.com/pages/courses: "Every course includes an assessment, with official badges issued through Accredible."
- https://academy.claude.com/courses: "Completion badge" is listed per course where it applies.

Recheck badges and lengths before relying on them; providers change their catalogues.
